What's under the interface

Mobile XD · the portfolio screenLive ↗

Why I built it

I wanted to see what my portfolio would look like as a mobile app.

I designed this to practise mobile UI and to produce specs a developer could build from, using my own portfolio as the content.

How it works

A 154-screen prototype in Adobe XD, with assets made in Illustrator and Photoshop.

A high-fidelity prototype in Adobe XD covering the home, about, portfolio and contact sections. Navigation stacks and interaction triggers are mapped out, the type scale and spacing stay consistent across screens, and grid alignments and component boundaries are marked for handoff.

Design decisions

A three-colour scheme

Charcoal and grey carry the layout, and peach is kept for the things you can act on, like the active tab and the Get Started button.

Bottom tab bar

The main sections stay one tap away and within reach of your thumb.

Specs for handoff

Grid alignments and component boundaries are defined so the screens could be built without guessing.

Challenges & reflections

  • Making interactive elements stand out through colour and detail, while keeping the interface simple enough to find your way around.
